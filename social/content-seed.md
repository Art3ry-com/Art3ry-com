# Art3ry — Content Seed (15 starters to prime the queue on day one)

Angles, not finished posts. Feed each into `/capture` (art3ry-os skill) —
one brain-dump becomes one blog + four platform drafts. The edge is the
**operator lens**: not *"here's what worked for us"* but *"here's how to
*see* what's leaking so you'd never miss it again."*

---

## Educational — how to actually look at a business (5)

1. **The four leaks every small business has.** Leads, money, time,
   follow-up. Which one you feel first tells you nothing about which is
   biggest.
2. **The missed-call math.** 20% of calls missed × $200 booking value ×
   50 calls/wk = $10K/wk walking out the door before you've paid rent.
3. **"Marketing is working" is not a metric.** The four numbers that
   actually tell you: leads/day, cost/lead, book-rate, revenue/job.
4. **The follow-up gap is where your competitors eat.** 80% of sales
   happen after the 5th touch. Most small businesses stop at 1.
5. **The dashboard trap.** More data ≠ better decisions. One number per
   lever, or none of them matter.

## Contrarian — hot takes with proof (3)

6. **You don't have a lead problem. You have a leak problem.** The leads
   are already there; they're just falling out. Show the funnel.
7. **You don't need more social media. You need every call answered.**
   Fix the receptionist before the reach.
8. **"Growth hacking" is a symptom of not knowing what your funnel is.**
   No funnel, no hacking. Draw the funnel first.

## Behind-the-scenes — the "done for you" made real (3)

9. **What an Art3ry diagnostic actually looks like.** 12 sections, one
   for each way a small business leaks. Show the report screen.
10. **The 5-minute check I do on any local business's Google presence.**
    Live-review a random one on camera.
11. **The system reading and drafting a follow-up at 11:47 PM.** The
    "done for you" made real — one clip, no narration needed.

## Case study — the canonical proof (2)

12. **How one company ran 700+ jobs with zero missed leads and no staff.**
    The exact stack. What each piece did. What broke first. What we fixed.
13. **The 100-day daily proof email.** Why we send it, what's on it, and
    why every operator should copy the format.

## Framework — how to look at it (2)

14. **The Reach → Contact → Invoice → Communicate → Deliver funnel.**
    Which stage yours leaks at, and how to tell without a dashboard.
15. **The "growth, done for you" test.** If you can't be gone for a week
    without revenue dropping, you don't have a business — you have a job.

## The one rule

**Log something every day**, even if it doesn't post. The queue is
compound interest.

- Miss a day of *logging* → you lose a day of the compounding.
- Miss a day of *posting* → nothing broke. The queue is still there.

Log first. Post second. The engine works because the raw material never
runs out.
