How I run it

The order matters. Ads poured onto a weak setup just make the leak bigger.

01

Fix the landing spot first

Your website, your Google profile and your follow-up have to catch the traffic before anyone pays to send it. That gets built first.

02

Organic before paid

Posts, short video, Google updates and content on a real schedule. It costs nothing per click and it compounds. Paid ads come in once we know which message works.

03

Pay only for what converts

Google Ads, Local Services Ads, Facebook and Instagram ads, set up with tracking from the first dollar, so you see cost per customer, not cost per click.

Questions

Should I run paid ads or organic marketing?
Both, in that order: organic first, paid second. Organic builds the name and tells us which message lands. Paid ads multiply what's already working. Starting with ads on an unproven setup is the most common way small businesses burn money.
Do you manage the ad spend?
Yes. I set up the campaigns, watch them, and cut what doesn't produce customers. The ad budget is paid by you directly to Google or Meta, so you always see exactly where it goes.
Can you teach me how to run it myself?
Yes. If you want to own it, I'll set it up and walk you through how it runs: what to post, how to read the numbers, and when to change an ad.
How do I know it's working?
Every channel is tracked back to calls, forms and paid invoices, and you get a plain-language report each month that says what worked, what didn't and what changes next.
