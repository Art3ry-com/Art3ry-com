How it goes

Three phases. In this order.

The order matters. Building before you know your market is just decorating, and launching without systems means it all lands back on you.

Phase 01

Know

Before a single thing gets built, I find out what's actually true about your market instead of guessing at it.

  • → Market research for your trade and your city
  • → Competitor breakdown: who ranks, who advertises, what they charge, where they're weak
  • → A straight read on where you stand today, including the things you won't want to hear
  • → What I'd do about it, in priority order
Phase 02

Build

The assets you should already own, built properly and wired to each other rather than bought separately.

  • → A website built to book work
  • → Professional email on your own domain
  • → Analytics installed and reading correctly, so later decisions have something behind them
  • → Google Business Profile built out properly
  • → Social accounts claimed, set up, and pointed back at you
  • → Local search foundations that compound instead of expiring
Phase 03

Operate

The phase everyone skips, and the reason most of these projects quietly die three months after launch.

  • → Intake, so nothing that comes in gets lost
  • → Invoicing that goes out same day
  • → Follow-up that doesn't need you to remember it
  • → Automation for the repetitive parts, including an optional assistant on your phone line if answering calls is your bottleneck
  • → A monthly report written in plain language
