Questions

How does ChatGPT decide which business to recommend?
It answers from what it can read: your website, your Google and Bing listings, reviews, directories and articles that mention you. Businesses that are easy to read, consistent everywhere and well reviewed get named. Businesses with a thin site and mismatched listings get skipped.
Is AI search different from SEO?
It's built on the same foundation, a fast site with real answers and a strong Google profile, plus a few things SEO alone doesn't cover: crawler access for AI tools, structured data, a plain-language summary file, and listings that agree with each other.
Can you guarantee my business shows up in ChatGPT?
No one can promise what an AI says, and anyone who does is selling you something. What I can do is build everything those tools look at, then check by asking them directly, in your city, the way your customers would.
Does this work for any kind of business?
Yes. Barbers, contractors, salons, law offices, restaurants, shops. If people ask who's the best at what you do in your city, this is how you become the answer.
