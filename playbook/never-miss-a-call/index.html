Never Miss a Call

The business that answers in five minutes beats the one that answers in five hours, almost every time. You cannot answer while you work. Something else can. This playbook covers what that something is, what it costs, and where it breaks.

Getting found is half the job. The other half is what happens when the phone rings and you are under a sink, on a roof, or with a client. Most owners lose more money here than anywhere else, and never see it happen, because a missed call leaves no receipt.

Start with the plain-English explainer, then the cost posts when you are comparing options, then the integration and DIY posts when you want to know how deep the water is.
