The origin

How an overloaded operator
built his own back office.

THE PROBLEM

Calls at midnight. Invoices sitting. Leads slipping.

My own company — a California field-services business — grew faster than one person could manage. Hiring wasn't the answer. The overhead was the problem, not the work itself.

THE BUILD

Every lead caught. Every invoice sent.

So I built the back office I couldn't afford to hire: intake that loses nothing, invoicing that fires on its own, follow-up that doesn't need reminding, and a search presence that compounds. It handled the overhead while I handled the work.

THE RESULT

700+ jobs. +60% traffic. No staff.

The system runs the overhead — leads, money, updates, search. I run the work. That gap between the old way and the new way is what I sell now.
