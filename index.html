The Growth Build

Three phases.
Know, build, operate.

Most people sell you one piece — a website, or some ads — and leave you to figure out the rest. This is the whole thing, in order, because the order is what makes it work.

PHASE 01

Know

Before I build anything, I find out what’s actually true about your market.

  • Market research for your trade and your city
  • Competitor breakdown — who’s beating you, and exactly why
  • An honest read on where you stand today
PHASE 02

Build

The assets you should already own, built properly and wired together.

  • A website built to book work, not to sit there
  • Professional email on your own domain
  • Analytics installed and actually reading right
  • Google Business Profile and local search
  • Social accounts stood up and claimed
PHASE 03

Operate

The part everyone skips. What keeps running after launch day.

  • Intake, so nothing that comes in gets lost
  • Invoicing and follow-up that don’t need reminding
  • Automation for the repetitive parts, including an optional assistant on your phone line
  • A monthly report you can actually read
