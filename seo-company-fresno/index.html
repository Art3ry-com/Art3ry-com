What actually moves local rankings

Google is unusually open about this. Its own guidance on improving local ranking comes down to three things: relevance, distance, and prominence. You can't move your shop, so the work is relevance and prominence — a complete Google Business Profile, real pages for each service and city, reviews arriving steadily, your name and number consistent everywhere, and structured data that tells Google exactly what kind of business you are.

None of that is a secret. It's just work nobody has time for while running an actual business. That's the whole reason we exist. We went deeper on the fundamentals in local SEO that actually works and on how we automate the cadence in local SEO automation.
