What actually moves local rankings

Google is unusually open about this. Its own guidance on improving local ranking comes down to three things: relevance, distance, and prominence. You can't move your shop, so the work is relevance and prominence: a complete Google Business Profile, real pages for each service and city, reviews arriving steadily, your name and number consistent everywhere, and structured data that tells Google exactly what kind of business you are.

None of that is a secret. It's just work nobody has time for while running an actual business. That's the whole reason I do this. I went deeper on the fundamentals in local SEO that actually works and on how I automate the cadence in local SEO automation.

The 90-day shape of it

Weeks one and two: audit you against your top competitors, fix the profile, fix anything on the site that's bleeding rankings. Weeks three through six: build the service and city pages that match what your customers search. Weeks seven through twelve: the cadence of content publishing, review flow, and profile activity. You see each piece as it ships, on your own site, not summarized in a report.

One thing I will not do is promise you a ranking. Nobody controls Google's results, and any company guaranteeing "#1 in Fresno" is selling a guess with a logo on it. I promise the work, the fastest wins first, and the numbers shown honestly.
