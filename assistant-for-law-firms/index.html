No. ART3RY drafts the intake reply, the consult confirmation, or the follow-up and shows it to you first. It only sends after your YES, so every client-facing word stays under your control.

No. It captures intake details and books consults, but it does not advise, evaluate, or make legal judgments. Those stay with you. ART3RY just makes sure the contact and the calendar are handled.
